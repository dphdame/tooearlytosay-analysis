Capitated Medicaid plans receive a fixed monthly payment per enrollee and bear claims risk. A common policy view holds that private plans screen out provider fraud and boost state convictions.

When we pair 15 years of state Medicaid Fraud Control Unit records with federal managed care series across all 50 states and DC, two findings stand out:

1. Within states, expanding managed care enrollment shows no detectable association with convictions per staff-year at state-year precision.

2. Across states, a descriptive three-stage accounting identity shows that case-to-indictment triage accounts for 75.4% of conviction variance, while state units open cases on just 23% of plan referrals.

That gap points to a testable hypothesis around referral documentation standards and forensic triage capacity.
