When a panel regression on a final outcome returns no detectable association, the aggregate slope alone cannot tell us where the production funnel varies.

When we write the outcome as a product of stage ratios and take natural logs, cross-unit variance becomes an exact covariance split that sums to 100%.

Applying that diagnostic to state Medicaid fraud enforcement records separates two analyses:

1. Within states, expanding managed care shows no detectable association with convictions per staff-year at state-year precision across both the 2010 to 2024 harmonized panel and the 2016 to 2024 comprehensive enrollment panel.

2. Across states over 2010 to 2024, a descriptive three-stage log-variance accounting identity shows that case-to-indictment triage accounts for 75.4% of log conviction variance.

Because that covariance split is descriptive, it does not prove why conversion varies, but it locates the stage where hypotheses about referral standards and triage capacity can be tested.
